Little Terminal privacy policy

Updated on November 6, 2026 · Português

In short

  • The account is optional (Google Play Games or Facebook), only to keep your progress in the cloud. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your progress is stored on this device (and in Android backup, if you keep it on).
  • Without an account, the game sends no data to us or to any advertising, analytics or payment company. With an account, the save goes to Unity (see Account).
  • The game does not use the Android advertising ID: the AD_ID permission is removed from the app.
  • There is no chat and no paid random item.
  • You can erase everything from inside the game: Pause › Settings › Delete my data.

Who we are

Little Terminal is a game by V-STACK Solutions, which is responsible for the data described here. Contact us at suporte@vstack-solutions.com.br.

What is stored on your device

WhatWhy
Game progress (in-game money, terminal level, staff, flights, missions)To continue where you left off.

This stays in the app's area on the device, protected by Android, and none of it reaches us. It is kept until you delete it from the game or uninstall.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android itself may copy your progress and settings there, so you do not lose the game when you change phones. That copy is held by Google under Google's policy and does not reach us either; you control it in Android's backup settings.

What leaves your device

  • From the game, nothing without an account. Without linking an account, the game uses no server, no usage analytics, no real ads and no purchases. Rewarded ads are optional and, in this version, simulated inside the game: no ad is loaded from the internet.
  • Unity. The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive anonymous technical statistics about the device, such as model, operating system and memory, to improve the engine. See Unity's policy.
  • Google Play. If you downloaded the game from Google Play, Google handles your account data under the Google privacy policy. Google may show us aggregated numbers and app crash reports (Android vitals) that do not identify you.
  • Links you tap. “Privacy policy” opens this page in your browser and “Support” opens your email app.

Account (optional)

You can link the game to a Google Play Games or Facebook account in Settings › Account, to keep your progress in the cloud and play on another device. Without linking, the game works the same, with the save on this device only. For this we use Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save (com.unity.services.authentication, com.unity.services.cloudsave): Unity creates a player ID and stores your save. With Google Play Games, Unity receives the code of your Google sign-in (the Play Games plugin uses the com.unity.modules.unitywebrequesttexture module for Google images; the game requests none). With Facebook, it receives the sign-in token, and the game asks only for the public profile: no email, no friends, no posting. We do not read or keep your name, photo or email. Our purchase server (through UnityWebRequest, module com.unity.modules.unitywebrequest) receives the account token to link purchases to it and keeps the purchase history (product and date) under a code derived from the ID, never the ID itself. The Facebook SDK runs with automatic events and the advertising ID turned off.

Delete: Settings › Delete my data deletes the account, the cloud save and the purchase history, and then the device data. Without the game installed: vvs2705.github.io/tiny-airport-empire/privacidade/excluir-conta/.

If you email support

We receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed to resolve your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask for access, correction, deletion and information about how your data is used, as provided by the LGPD in Brazil, the Data Privacy Act in the Philippines and the Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) in Indonesia.

  • In the game: Pause › Settings › Delete my data erases your progress, settings, tutorial steps seen and the diary, after a confirmation, and closes the game. When you open it again, it starts from scratch. Uninstalling the game also erases everything.
  • Exported copies: a diary you exported stays wherever you saved or shared it; delete it there.
  • Any other request: email suporte@vstack-solutions.com.br. We reply within 15 days.

Children and teenagers

Little Terminal is for ages 13 and up and is not part of the Google Play Families program. Because teenagers may play it, it ships in its most protective mode: no account, no chat, no personalized ads, no advertising ID and no paid random items. If a child has emailed us personal data, let us know and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If a new version starts collecting anything, such as usage statistics, real ads or purchases, this page changes before that version reaches the store: it will say what is added, why and with whom, and the date at the top will change.

For reviewers: third-party packages that send data: none. Network access in the game code: none. Permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: removed. Permissions requested from the player: none (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or notifications).
