Delete your Little Terminal account

Updated on November 5, 2026 · Português · Privacy policy

What is deleted

The game account is optional: it only exists if you linked Little Terminal to Google Play Games or Facebook in Settings › Account. Deleting removes, with no copy left:

  • the game account and the login linked to it;
  • the progress saved in the cloud;
  • the account's purchase history on our server (product and date).

What stays: the record of each purchase that Google Play keeps (payment, refund), under Google's policy, and a code for each purchase on our server, which does not point to you or to the account and only stops the same purchase from being credited twice. Your Google Play purchases are still yours and can be restored.

In the game (right away)

  1. Open Little Terminal and tap Pause › Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm with Erase all.

It needs internet. The game deletes the account, the cloud save and the history, then erases this device and closes. If you no longer have the game, install it again, sign in with the same login in Settings › Account and follow the steps above.

By email

Write to suporte@vstack-solutions.com.br with the subject “Delete Little Terminal account”. Tell us which login you used (Google Play Games or Facebook) and, if you have it, the Account ID shown in Settings › Account. We delete it within 15 days and reply when done. Do not send passwords.
